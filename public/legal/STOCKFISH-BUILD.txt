Rebuild (not required for building Little Chess):
1. Extract the source archive.
2. Install Emscripten and its dependencies, Node.js, make, and a C++ build toolchain.
3. See build.js in the archive for the required Emscripten version and flags.
4. Copy nn-61e7af4bb97d.nnue into the extracted src/ directory.
5. From the extracted root, run:
   node build.js --lite --single-threaded --no-split
6. The matching .js and .wasm must be served side by side.
